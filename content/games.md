## Fira’s Pursuit

- Fast auto-runner. Fira runs. The player reads the next obstacle and transforms before impact. Wrong form, or too late, and the run restarts.
- Art direction, from the itch page: a child’s notebook, crayon and pencil.
- Controls: left thumb holds a radial wheel of animals. Right thumb uses the current form.
- Godot, HTML5, in development. Jam: GAMESEED 2026, mobile. Author page: Seed Academy.
- Role: game designer.
- Team: Danu Raditya (art), Marleen Pangestu (UI and art), Anabel Oklana (narrative), Owen Limantoro and Mark Lorens (development).
- The itch page marks the code as AI-assisted.
- Link: https://seedacademy.itch.io/firas-pursuit
- Media on the page is what Kean supplied on 25 November 2026: a four-second demo, a glide still, and a corridor still. Do not swap them for other Fira art.

## Space Opurrator

- Published line: “Two evil cats. One tiny spaceship. Zero personal space.”
- Two-player co-op on two iPhones. Same Wi-Fi or a hotspot. No single player.
- Shared view of a cockpit about four screens wide. One player moving the view moves both.
- Loop: find a coded target, decode it from a sheet on the other side, agree on the buttons, enter them together, destroy it, repeat. Satisfaction at zero is the loss.
- Prototype. Very Disco Game Jam 2026.3. Godot (public repo `MarkLorens/SpaceOpurrator`; the Godot project name is SpaceOpurraider — do not print that name).
- Design: Kennard Mahib, Amanda Dorotea. Art: Danu Raditya, Natchaya Futragoon. Code: Mark Pardede, Owen Limantoro.
- The itch page marks the code as AI-assisted.
- Link: https://seedacademy.itch.io/space-opurrator-game
- Media on the page is what Kean supplied on 25 November 2026: a demo of the ready screen through the cockpit, and the pixel title screen. Do not swap them for other Space Opurrator art.
